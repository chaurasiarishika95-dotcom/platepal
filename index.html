How to use PlatePal

  • Say what you want in your own words: "veg dinner for 2 under ₹500", "something spicy, no nuts".
  • Refine as you go: "cheaper", "less spicy", "make it Chinese", "I'm allergic to dairy".
  • Say "start over" to clear your preferences.
  • Diet, allergies and budget are never relaxed. If nothing fits, I'll tell you which softer preference I loosened.
  • AI understands your request; fixed rules choose the dishes, so I only suggest dishes that are really on the menu.
